# Vayishlach: learning and gameplay review

## Learning outline
Ages 5-6: returning home; Yaakov and Esav; making peace with gifts; praying when afraid; Rachel and Binyamin. Familiar answers are intentional.
Ages 10-11: preparation for danger (400 men, two camps); wrestling and the name Yisrael; the gid hanasheh; consideration for children and animals; return to Beis El; Dinah and the brothers' response; Yitzchak's burial by both sons.
Messages are discussed rather than all forced into character riddles. Prayer alongside practical effort is the back-page discussion. Care for slower travelers is a situation question and acting prompt.

## Gameplay review
Opening clues allow several possibilities; subsequent clues narrow them. Some early guesses remain possible, especially for knowledgeable players. No first-letter hints or answers explicitly stated. Accept equivalent answers, especially prayer, gifts and the care-for-travelers question. The last three questions vary the format to avoid forcing personification. 15 questions preserve the established print layout; difficulty is a first estimate to calibrate after play. Parents can alternate age-appropriate questions and offer 3/2/1 points by clue, with no penalty for a thoughtful wrong guess.
